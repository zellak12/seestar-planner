Site, darkness and tree horizon

Views: Month lists targets at their best in each window; Night is a Staralt-style plot for one date; Year shows one target's height through every night of the year. How “best month” works: for each night, time above your horizon between full darkness and the session end, weighted by 1/airmass; a target is listed in the month window where that is highest. Each window is the month plus the first days of the next. Target altitudes use J2000 positions precessed to date (no refraction, good to about 0.1°).
